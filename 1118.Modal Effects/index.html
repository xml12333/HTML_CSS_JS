Unrolled Scroll

This modal opens from left to right like an unfolding parchment. The effect uses rotateY().

Reverse Roll

Reverse Roll

Slide Roll R → L

Spring

Spring effect with a bounce! The modal starts at scale(0) and bounces to scale(1) with a cubic-bezier(0.68, -0.55, 0.27, 1.55).

Zoom Rotate

Zoom while rotating effect! The modal goes from scale(0) rotate(45deg) to scale(1) rotate(0deg).

3D

Depth effect! The modal comes from far away translateZ(-300px) rotateX(30deg) to the center.

Origami L → R

Diagonal unfolding from the left effect! The modal unfolds from rotate(-90deg) scale(0.3) skewX(20deg) to center.

Bounce

Bounce effect! The modal arrives with a bounceIn animation of 0.8s.

Diagonal Top Left

Diagonal slide from top left effect! The modal slides from translate3d(-200%, -200%, 0) rotate(-45deg) to center.

Convex

Convex swelling effect! The modal starts as a small round bubble and inflates like a balloon to reach its final shape with a relief effect.

Diagonal Top Right

Diagonal Bottom Right

Flip X

The modal flips around the X axis with rotateX(180deg).

Flip Y + Scale

Combines rotateY(180deg) and scale(0.3) for a dynamic effect. The flip goes from left to right.

Flip Y ← Scale

Combines rotateY(-180deg) and scale(0.3). The flip goes from right to left.

Flip Mix

Double flip effect! The modal flips on both axes with rotateX(180deg) rotateY(180deg) scale(0.3).
